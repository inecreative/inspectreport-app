HomeGauge Pricing Explained: What Inspectors Actually Pay

November 17, 2026 · InspectReport AI

HomeGauge's pricing is refreshingly simple compared with most home inspection report software: one product, one price, one trial. There are no tiers to compare and no per-inspector surcharge spelled out on the pricing page. If you are budgeting for the year, the number you need is $89 a month — but the details around that number are worth knowing before you sign up.

The short answer: as of November 2026, HomeGauge Software costs $89 a month after a 30-day free trial, and it is $0 to start. There is no annual billing option listed on HomeGauge's own pricing page — no annual price, no yearly discount, no monthly/annual toggle. A solo inspector pays $89 a month, or $1,068 a year, for as long as they stay on monthly billing.

The formula

HomeGauge's pricing page states it plainly: a 30-day free trial, $0 to start, then $89 a month. That is the whole price list for the software itself. Unlike Spectora, whose page lists $109 a month plus $99 a month for each additional inspector, HomeGauge's page publishes no per-inspector add-on price and no team pricing at all.

So the monthly bill is:

$89, every month, on monthly billing

And the annual cost is:

$89 × 12 = $1,068 a year

There is no annual plan to discount that figure. If you are used to software that knocks two months off for paying yearly, HomeGauge does not offer that on its pricing page — the price is the price, month after month.

What 1-, 3-, and 5-person shops actually pay

Because HomeGauge publishes a single flat rate with no multi-inspector pricing on its page, the honest answer for teams is that the page does not say. Here is what is actually published:

That gap matters for budgeting. Spectora's team math is public — $307 a month for three inspectors, $505 for five — so a growing shop can price the move before talking to anyone. With HomeGauge, a multi-inspector firm has to ask sales what extra seats cost, because the page does not say. If you run a team, get that number in writing before you compare HomeGauge with anything else.

What the $89 a month includes

The price covers the full HomeGauge workflow: the Desktop Report Writer for Windows and Mac, the Companion mobile app for collecting photos and data in the field, online report hosting, and the comment library (CRL) for reusable narratives. The workflow is desktop-first by design — you collect in the field, then finish and format the report at your desk, with custom templates and several report formats.

That desktop-first workflow is the real dividing line. Inspectors who like a full keyboard and a big screen for the write-up, and who have used HomeGauge for years, tend to stay because the tool fits how they already work. Inspectors who want the report finished before they leave the driveway will find the desktop step an extra stop.

There is also an optional add-on worth knowing about: HomeGauge Protect, a guarantee product priced per inspection, which you can switch off. It is not part of the $89 subscription, so do not count it in the monthly math — but if you turn it on, it adds a per-inspection cost on top of the subscription.

HomeGauge and Spectora are now one company

One thing that confuses inspectors comparing prices: HomeGauge and Spectora are now owned by the same company. Spectora acquired HomeGauge on April 1, 2025, and HomeGauge's own pricing page now sells the two products side by side — HomeGauge Software at $89 a month, Spectora Software at $109 a month. Both continue to operate as separate products with their own pricing, and neither page requires you to switch.

So the choice is not really between two companies any more. It is between two workflows from the same owner: HomeGauge's desktop report writer at $89 a month, or Spectora's cloud software at $109 a month with a $99-a-month charge for each additional inspector. If you are already on one of them, the other is one pricing page away — but switching still means learning a new workflow and rebuilding your templates.

Who should stay with HomeGauge

Honestly, plenty of inspectors should. If your templates, comment library, and report formats already live in HomeGauge, if your office workflow is built around finishing reports at a desk, and if you are happy with the reports you send, $89 a month is a fair price for a mature tool — and switching would cost you weeks of rebuilding for savings you may not feel. The cheapest software is rarely worth it if it makes your busiest day slower.

HomeGauge is the weaker choice if you are starting from scratch, if you finish reports in the field and resent the desktop step, or if the monthly bill is the deciding factor. A new inspector choosing between $89 a month and $9 a month flat, with no per-inspector fees, saves $960 in the first year alone — money that buys a lot of marketing, or a second camera, or simply a lighter month.

The bottom line

HomeGauge pricing is one flat number: $89 a month after a 30-day free trial, $0 to start, with no annual discount on the page and no published team pricing. It is $20 a month cheaper than Spectora's solo price and $80 a month more than InspectReport AI's flat $9. The price is simple; the decision is about workflow. If you love finishing reports at a desk with a tool you already know, HomeGauge's price is easy to justify. If you are choosing fresh and want the report done before you leave the driveway, the $80-a-month gap is worth a look.
