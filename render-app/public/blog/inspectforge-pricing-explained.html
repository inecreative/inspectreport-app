InspectForge Pricing Explained: What Inspectors Actually Pay

November 18, 2026 · InspectReport AI

InspectForge is the newest of the three names inspectors usually compare when shopping for home inspection report software — and its pricing has a twist the other two do not: a 12-month introductory price for InterNACHI members that steps up afterwards. If you only look at the first-year number, you will budget wrong for year two.

The short answer: as of November 2026, InterNACHI members pay $25 a month for the solo Starter plan, $35 for Pro (up to 3 inspectors), or $49 for Business (up to 10 inspectors) for the first 12 months. After that, standard pricing applies — $39, $69, and $99 a month — and that rate is locked for 2 full years of continuous subscription. There is a 30-day free trial with no credit card required, and no per-inspection fees on any plan.

The formula: a promo year, then a step up

InterNACHI's vendor-offer page for InspectForge states the deal plainly: exclusive member pricing for the first 12 months, then standard pricing. The three tiers are priced by headcount, not by add-on seats:

PlanFirst 12 months (InterNACHI)Standard price afterWho it covers
Starter$25/mo$39/moSolo inspector
Pro$35/mo$69/moUp to 3 inspectors, unlimited inspections, AI report summaries
Business$49/mo$99/moUp to 10 inspectors, white-label, AI photo analysis

One small footnote: InspectForge's own website lists the standard tiers as $39.99, $69.99, and $99.99 a month — a cent above the round numbers on the InterNACHI page. Either way, the shape is the same: the promo saves $14 a month on Starter, $34 on Pro, and $50 on Business, and then the bill steps up.

So the annual math, per tier:

Starter: $25 × 12 = $300 in year one, then $39 × 12 = $468 a year
Pro: $35 × 12 = $420 in year one, then $69 × 12 = $828 a year
Business: $49 × 12 = $588 in year one, then $99 × 12 = $1,188 a year

That step-up is the number to put in your budget. A solo inspector who joins on the $25 promo and forgets the second year is looking at a 56% increase in month thirteen — $25 to $39. The two-year rate lock softens it: once the standard price kicks in, it is locked for 2 full years of continuous subscription even if public pricing rises. But the lock starts after the promo, not instead of it.

What 1-, 3-, and 5-person shops actually pay

Unlike Spectora, which charges $109 a month plus $99 a month per additional inspector, InspectForge prices by tier caps — you move up a tier when your headcount passes the limit, not per seat. Here is the published math:

Solo inspectorStarter$25$39$468

That tier structure is genuinely good value for a 3-person shop in year one: $35 a month for three inspectors with unlimited inspections is $11.67 per inspector, far below Spectora's $307 a month for the same three. Even at the standard $69, a 3-person shop pays less than a quarter of Spectora's team price. The catch is the same as everywhere else in this pricing: the cheap year is year one only.

There is no annual billing option mentioned on the InterNACHI offer page — no yearly price, no pay-up-front discount. The price is monthly, and the only discount on the page is the 12-month member promo itself.

What the price includes — and where AI sits

Every plan includes the full InspectForge platform: a mobile-first app on iOS and Android with full offline mode, a desktop web app with automatic sync, customizable report output with PDF and an online client portal, digital pre-inspection agreements with e-signatures, built-in scheduling with a calendar and route-optimized maps view, a CRM with agent referral tracking, payment collection, a free public website with an online booking widget, and free template migration from Spectora, HomeGauge, HIP, Palm-Tech, or any other platform. No per-inspection fees, ever.

The AI features are where the tiers split. All plans get AI Quick Add — snap a photo and the AI identifies the defect and places it in your report — plus voice input and the MI6 AI assistant that writes recommendations and generates report summaries. But AI photo analysis, the feature that reads a photo and turns it into a finding, lives only on the Business tier. Pro gets AI report summaries; Starter gets the basic AI tools without photo analysis.

So the real question is not "which tier is cheapest" but "which AI do I need." A solo inspector who wants AI photo analysis pays $49 a month in year one and $99 a month after — not $25. The $25 Starter price is for a solo who is happy with AI-assisted summaries and manual photo findings.

The gap between InspectForge's standard Business tier and InspectReport AI is $90 a month, or $1,080 a year — the same AI photo-analysis job, one at $99 a month and one at $9 a month flat per account with no per-inspector fees. Even the promo-year Starter at $25 a month is nearly three times InspectReport AI's $9. That is the honest price comparison today.

But the price comparison is not the whole comparison. InspectForge is a full inspection platform — scheduling, CRM, payments, agreements, a public website — while InspectReport AI does one job: turn your site photos and short notes into a finished report with severity badges and repair-cost estimates, plus priced estimates for repair pros. If you need the platform, the platform price is the price. If you only need the report, the $9 flat rate is the cheaper way to get it.

InspectForge's 30-day trial is also a real advantage over InspectReport AI's 7-day trial: a full month to move templates over and run real inspections through it before paying, with no card required either way.

A note on the name: InspectForge is not InsForge

One trap worth flagging while you search: "InsForge" (insforge.dev) is a different company entirely — an open-source backend platform for AI coding tools. Its pricing has nothing to do with InspectForge inspection software. If a search result shows developer-tool pricing next to the InspectForge name, you are looking at the wrong company. The prices in this post come from InterNACHI's vendor-offer page for InspectForge and InspectForge's own website.

Who should stay with InspectForge

Honestly, plenty of inspectors should. If you use the scheduling, CRM, payments, and website that come with the subscription, if your templates already live there, and if the promo-year price fits your budget with the step-up planned for, $39 to $99 a month is a fair price for a full platform — and switching to save money would cost you the tools you actually use every day. A 3-person shop paying $69 a month standard for Pro is getting a good deal by any measure in this market.

InspectForge is the weaker choice if you are a solo inspector who only needs the report itself, if you want AI photo analysis without paying for the top tier, or if the year-two step-up breaks the budget you set in year one. A solo inspector on the Business tier after the promo pays $99 a month — $1,188 a year — for AI photo analysis that a $9-a-month flat tool includes for every account.

The bottom line

InspectForge pricing is a 12-month member promo — $25, $35, or $49 a month by headcount — that steps up to $39, $69, or $99 a month, locked for two years. It is the cheapest full platform in year one for a small team, and the most expensive way to get AI photo analysis once the promo ends. The price is honest and published; the decision is about whether you need the platform or just the report. If you need the platform, budget for year two, not year one. If you only need the report, the $90-a-month gap to a $9 flat rate is worth a look.
