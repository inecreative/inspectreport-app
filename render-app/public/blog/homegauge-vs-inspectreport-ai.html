HomeGauge vs InspectReport AI: An Honest Comparison (2026)

October 31, 2026 · InspectReport AI

HomeGauge has been a fixture in home inspection software for a long time, and plenty of inspectors have built their entire reporting workflow around it. InspectReport AI is a newer, narrower tool: a web-based report writer that turns photos and short notes into a structured report with severity badges, repair-cost estimates, and PDF export. This comparison uses published prices as of October 2026 and is honest about where HomeGauge still wins — starting with the loyalty of inspectors who know it inside out.

One piece of context first: Spectora acquired HomeGauge in April 2025. Both brands still operate separately today, and HomeGauge's own site describes it as part of the same family. That does not change the day-to-day comparison below, but it is worth knowing when you are choosing software you may use for years.

The short version

HomeGauge costs $89/mo flat. InspectReport AI costs $49/mo flat per account. Both are flat prices with no per-inspector surcharge, so the pricing question is simple: a $40/mo difference, about $480 a year. The bigger difference is how you work. HomeGauge's report writer is a Windows desktop application. InspectReport AI runs in a browser, on your phone at the property and on any computer — Mac or PC — back at the office.

Pricing: two flat prices, $40 apart

Unlike per-seat tools, neither product raises your bill when you add an inspector. HomeGauge is $89/mo flat. InspectReport AI is $49/mo flat per account, with no per-inspector fees and no introductory promo that doubles later. Over a year, the difference is roughly $480 — real money for a small operation, though for most inspectors the workflow difference below will matter more than the price gap.

Desktop app versus web app

HomeGauge: the desktop writer

HomeGauge's report writer is a Windows desktop application. Inspectors who have used it for years have deep template libraries and a workflow that lives on one machine, often with an offline-friendly way of working. If you are on Windows and your templates are dialed in, that setup has genuine value. The friction appears at the edges: Mac users need a virtual machine to run the writer, and the report is tied to the desktop rather than starting on the phone in your hand at the property.

InspectReport AI: start on site, finish anywhere

InspectReport AI runs in a web browser, so the report starts where the inspection happens. You photograph findings and type short notes on your phone, the AI structures them into findings with severity badges and repair-cost estimates, and the live preview builds as you go. Anything you start on site can be finished on a Mac, a PC, or a tablet — there is nothing to install and no single machine that owns your report.

Trial terms

HomeGauge offers a 30-day free trial, which is generous in length. Note that HomeGauge's own trial page describes the service as automatically recurring, so mark the renewal date if you test it. InspectReport AI's trial is shorter but wider open at the start: every new account gets a free 3-day Pro trial with full access including PDF export, and no card required. After the trial, reports and the live preview remain free; the $49/mo plan keeps PDF export.

Who should stay with HomeGauge

  • You love the desktop report writer and your template library is years in the making.
  • Your workflow is offline-first and centered on one Windows machine.
  • You are a long-time user whose clients and agents already know your HomeGauge reports.
  • Thirty days of trial time matters more to you than starting on your phone.

Switching software has a real cost in relearning and rebuilding templates. If HomeGauge's writer is the backbone of a workflow that works, the $40/mo difference alone is not a reason to move.

Who fits InspectReport AI

  • You are on a Mac, or you are tired of keeping a Windows setup alive for one program.
  • You want the report to start on site, from photos and notes on your phone.
  • You would rather review AI-structured findings than build every report section by hand.
  • You want flat, simple pricing: $49/mo per account, with reports and preview free.
