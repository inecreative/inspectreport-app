Spectora Pricing Explained: What 1-, 3-, and 5-Person Shops Actually Pay

November 7, 2026 · InspectReport AI

Spectora's headline price is $109 a month — but that is the price for one inspector. Every additional inspector adds $99 a month, so the bill for a team looks nothing like the headline. If you are budgeting for a shop rather than a solo practice, price the team you actually have.

The short answer: as of November 2026, Spectora costs $109 a month for a solo inspector, $307 a month for a 3-person shop, and $505 a month for a 5-person shop on monthly billing — before the optional Advanced add-on. On annual billing the same shops pay $1,090, $3,088, and $5,086 a year. The per-inspector charge, not the base price, is what decides the bill.

The formula

Spectora's own support pages state the pricing plainly: a monthly subscription is $109 per month, an annual subscription is $1,090 per year, and add-on inspectors are $99 per month or $999 a year each. There is no pay-per-inspection option for the base subscription — monthly and annual only.

So the monthly bill is:

$109 + ($99 × additional inspectors)

And the annual bill is:

$1,090 + ($999 × additional inspectors)

The add-on that changes the bill: Spectora Advanced

The base price is not the whole story. Spectora Advanced — automations, CRM, and metrics — is an optional add-on at $4 per published inspection, as of November 2026. It is charged per inspection, so it scales with volume, not headcount:

  • Solo inspector, 20 inspections a month: $109 + (20 × $4) = $189/mo on monthly billing.
  • 3-person shop, 60 inspections a month: $307 + (60 × $4) = $547/mo on monthly billing.
  • 5-person shop, 100 inspections a month: $505 + (100 × $4) = $905/mo on monthly billing.

A busy month costs more than a slow month. When you compare Spectora with a flat-priced tool, multiply the $4 by your real monthly volume — your best month, not your slowest.

Two things that are not the standard price

Promo and migration offers. Spectora runs limited-time offers — for example, a migration offer for Horizon users at $55 a month for 6 months (or $545 for the first year) — after which standard pricing applies. Those rates are not the general price, and budgeting with them means budgeting for a price rise in month seven. Always ask what the price becomes when the intro period ends, and write that number into your budget.

The free trial. Spectora's trial is 2 free inspections with no card and no time limit. That is a genuinely useful trial: two full reports on real jobs before you pay anything. Use it to test the whole workflow — scheduling, report writing, and delivery — not just the report writer.

What the per-inspector price actually buys

The honest case for Spectora is scope. It is not only a report writer: scheduling, payments, CRM, and client communication live in the same system. A multi-inspector firm that runs its whole operation inside Spectora may find the per-inspector price worth it, because replacing those pieces separately has its own cost and friction.

The honest case against it is the same fact from the other side. If your scheduling, payments, and marketing already live elsewhere and you mainly need reports written and delivered as PDFs, you are paying a per-inspector platform price for a report-writing job — and the bill triples as you hire, even though the report work per inspector stays the same.

FAQ

How much does Spectora cost for a solo home inspector?

$109 a month on monthly billing, or $1,090 a year on annual billing, as of November 2026. The optional Advanced add-on costs $4 per published inspection on top of that.

How much does a 3-person inspection firm pay for Spectora?

$307 a month on monthly billing: $109 for the first inspector plus $99 for each of the two additional inspectors. That is $3,684 a year, or $3,088 a year on annual billing ($1,090 + 2 × $999) — before the optional $4-per-inspection Advanced add-on.

How much does a 5-person firm pay for Spectora?

$505 a month on monthly billing: $109 plus $99 for each of the four additional inspectors. That is $6,060 a year, or $5,086 a year on annual billing — before Advanced.

Does Spectora offer a free trial?

Yes: 2 free inspections, no card required, and no time limit. You can test it on real jobs before paying.

Is there a pay-per-inspection plan?

No. Spectora states that it offers monthly and annual subscriptions only, with no pay-per-inspection option for the base plan. The $4-per-inspection charge applies only to the optional Advanced add-on.

All Spectora prices in this post are from Spectora's own support pages, checked November 2026. Prices can change — confirm the current figures on Spectora's site before you decide.
