How Much Does Home Inspection Software Cost in 2026? Vendor-by-Vendor Math

November 3, 2026 · InspectReport AI

Home inspection software does not have one price. The same tool can cost a solo inspector $109 a month and a five-person firm more than $500 a month, before any per-inspection add-on. If you are comparing tools, compare the bill you will actually pay at your team size — not the headline number on the pricing page.

The short answer: as of November 2026, a solo inspector pays roughly $9 to $109 a month for the major report tools, a three-person shop pays $9 to $307 a month, and a five-person shop pays $9 to $505 a month — before optional per-inspection fees. The spread is almost entirely about how each vendor charges for extra inspectors.

How inspection software is priced

Most tools use one of three models, and the model matters more than the starting price:

  • Flat per account. One price no matter how many inspectors use the account. The bill does not grow when you add a team member.
  • Per inspector. A base price for the first inspector, plus a fixed fee for each additional inspector. This is where small teams get surprised.
  • Base plus per-inspection. A monthly base price, plus a fee on every published report for an optional add-on tier. Your bill rises with volume, even in a good month.

A fourth cost is the promo price: a lower rate for a fixed first period, after which the standard rate applies. Always ask what the price becomes in year two, and write that number — not the promo number — into your budget.

InspectReport AI: $9/mo flat per account

InspectReport AI costs $9 a month flat per account as of November 2026, with no per-inspector fees and no per-inspection fees. That is $108 a year whether the account serves one inspector or a small team. Every new account also gets a free 7-day Pro trial with full access including PDF export, and no card is required; accounts, reports, and the live preview stay free even after the trial ends. Its limit is scope: it is a report writer, not a full scheduling and CRM platform.

Spectora: $109/mo, plus $99/mo per additional inspector

Spectora's base price is $109 a month ($1,090 a year if paid annually) as of November 2026, and each additional inspector costs $99 a month ($999 a year). The arithmetic is unforgiving for teams:

  • Solo: $109/mo — $1,308 a year on monthly billing.
  • 3-person shop: $109 + (2 × $99) = $307/mo — $3,684 a year.
  • 5-person shop: $109 + (4 × $99) = $505/mo — $6,060 a year.

Then there is the optional Advanced add-on at $4 per published inspection. At 20 inspections a month, that adds $80 a month — so a solo inspector on Advanced pays $189 a month. Spectora's trial is 2 free inspections with no card and no time limit. Our full breakdown is on the Spectora alternative page.

HomeGauge: $89/mo flat

HomeGauge costs $89 a month flat as of November 2026, with a 30-day free trial. That is $1,068 a year, and — unlike Spectora — the flat rate does not multiply as you add inspectors. The trade-off is platform: the report writer is a Windows desktop app, so Mac users need a virtual machine, and there is no free version. See our HomeGauge alternative page for the full comparison.

InspectForge: cheap in year one, then the standard rate

InspectForge's InterNACHI-member offer is the clearest example of why promo pricing needs a second look. For the first 12 months, members pay $25 a month for Starter (solo), $35 for Pro (up to 3 inspectors), or $49 for Business (up to 10 inspectors, white label, AI photo analysis). After 12 months, standard pricing applies — $39, $69, and $99 a month respectively — locked for 2 years of continuous subscription. There is also a 30-day free trial with no card.

Budget it honestly: a solo inspector pays $300 in year one on Starter, then $468 a year after that — more than four times InspectReport AI's $108 a year flat. A Business-tier firm pays $588 in year one, then $1,188 a year, about eleven times InspectReport AI's flat $108. The promo is real value; it just is not the long-term price. What the monthly price does not include

Before you sign up, check five things that routinely add to the bill:

  1. Extra seats. Ask for the price at your actual headcount, including the person you plan to hire next quarter.
  2. Per-inspection fees. Multiply any per-report add-on by your real monthly volume, not your slowest month.
  3. Annual versus monthly billing. Spectora's annual price saves $218 a year for the base plan versus monthly billing — but only if you stay the full year.
  4. Promo expiry. Write down the post-promo price and the date it starts. A $25-a-month tool that becomes $39 is still cheap; a $49 tool that becomes $99 is a different purchase.
  5. Platform costs. A Windows-only writer on a Mac team, or a tool that needs a separate scheduling app, carries costs the subscription price never shows.

How to choose without overpaying

Start from what you actually use. If you write reports and hand clients a PDF — and your scheduling, payments, and marketing already live elsewhere — a flat report writer at $9 or $89 a month covers the job, and paying $307 a month for a platform mostly buys unused features. If you run a multi-inspector firm that lives inside one system for scheduling, CRM, payments, and reports, the per-inspector price may be worth it, because replacing those pieces separately has its own cost and friction.

Run the cheapest honest test: take your last month's inspection count and your actual team size, price each tool with the formulas above, and add any per-inspection fee. Then try the tool on a real job before committing — a trial covering one full report tells you more than any pricing page. FAQ

What is the cheapest home inspection software for a solo inspector?

As of November 2026, InspectReport AI is cheapest at $9 a month flat per account. Next is InspectForge Starter at $39 a month after its first-year InterNACHI promo ($25 a month in year one), followed by HomeGauge at $89 and Spectora at $109.

What does a 3-person inspection firm actually pay for Spectora?

$307 a month on monthly billing: $109 for the first inspector plus $99 for each of the two additional inspectors. That is $3,684 a year, before the optional $4-per-inspection Advanced add-on.

Do any tools charge per inspection?

Yes, as an optional add-on rather than the base price. Spectora Advanced costs $4 per published inspection on top of the monthly subscription. Other tools charge per report as their main model, so always check whether the price you see is per month, per report, or both.

Is a free trial really free?

It depends on the vendor. Spectora offers 2 free inspections with no card and no time limit; InspectForge offers 30 days with no card; InspectReport AI offers 7 days of full Pro access with no card. HomeGauge's trial page describes its 30-day trial as automatically recurring, so check its cancellation terms before starting it.
