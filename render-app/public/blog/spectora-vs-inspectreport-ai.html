Spectora vs InspectReport AI: An Honest Comparison (2026)

November 3, 2026 · InspectReport AI

Spectora and InspectReport AI both help home inspectors produce reports, but they are built for different jobs. Spectora is a full business platform for running an inspection company. InspectReport AI is a focused report writer: you add photos and short notes, and it structures them into a finished report with severity badges, repair-cost estimates, and PDF export. This comparison uses published prices as of October 2026 and tries to be fair to both sides — including the cases where Spectora is plainly the better choice.

The short version

If you need scheduling, online payments, agent management, and a system that runs your whole inspection business, Spectora is the more complete product and its price reflects that. If you are a solo inspector or a small team whose main pain is the report itself — the hours of retyping notes at night — InspectReport AI covers that job for $9/mo flat per account, with no per-inspector fees. Many inspectors do not need a platform; they need their evenings back.

Pricing: where the math diverges

Solo inspector

For a one-person operation, Spectora costs $109/mo for the base plan. InspectReport AI costs $9/mo flat. That is a $100/mo difference, or about $1,200 a year, for the report-writing job both tools handle. Spectora also offers an optional Advanced add-on at $4 per inspection, which adds up on top of the base price if you use it.

A three-person shop

The gap widens with team size because the two products price differently. Spectora charges $109/mo base plus $99/mo for each additional inspector, so a three-person shop pays $109 + $99 + $99 = $307/mo. InspectReport AI charges $9/mo flat per account regardless of how many inspectors are on it — there are no per-inspector fees and no introductory promo price that doubles after a year. For a three-person shop, the difference is roughly $298/mo, or about $3,576 a year.

What each product actually is

Spectora: a platform for running the business

Spectora aims to be the operating system for an inspection company. Beyond report writing, it covers scheduling, agreements, payments, and communication with agents and clients. For a growing multi-inspector firm, having those pieces in one system is a real advantage, and it is what the higher price buys. The trade-off is cost and weight: you pay for the whole platform whether or not you use all of it, and per-inspector pricing means the bill grows with every hire.

InspectReport AI: a report writer, deliberately narrow

InspectReport AI does one job. On site, you take photos and type short notes the way you would say them out loud. The AI structures those into findings grouped by area, each with a severity badge, a plain-language description, and a repair-cost estimate. You review and edit everything, watch the report build in a live preview, and export a clean PDF. It runs in a web browser on your phone and desktop, so there is nothing to install. It does not do scheduling, payments, or CRM — if you already handle those another way, you are not paying twice for them.

Trial terms

Spectora's trial is two free inspections with no credit card and no time limit, which is a genuinely low-pressure way to test it on real jobs. InspectReport AI gives every new account a free 7-day Pro trial with full access, including PDF export, and no card required. Accounts, reports, and the live preview stay free after the trial; subscribing at $9/mo simply keeps PDF export. Either way, you can judge both tools on your own inspections before paying anything.

Who should stay with Spectora

  • You need scheduling, online payments, and agent/client communication in one system.
  • You run a larger team and rely on platform-level features to coordinate inspectors.
  • Your whole workflow is already built around Spectora and switching costs would be real.
  • You use and value the optional Advanced features enough to justify the per-inspection add-on.

There is no honest version of this comparison where a full platform and a focused report writer are interchangeable. If the platform features are load-bearing for your business, stay.

Who fits InspectReport AI

  • You are a solo inspector or a small team, and report writing is the bottleneck.
  • You already book jobs and take payments another way and do not want to pay for a platform.
  • Per-inspector pricing stings — your software bill should not rise every time you add help.
  • You want to start free, test on a real job during the trial, and decide from the finished PDF.

FAQ

Is InspectReport AI a replacement for Spectora?

Only for the report-writing part of the job. InspectReport AI replaces the hours spent turning photos and notes into a finished report and PDF. It does not replace scheduling, payments, or CRM features, so inspectors who depend on those in Spectora should keep them.

How much does a three-person shop save with InspectReport AI?

At prices as of November 2026, Spectora costs a three-person shop $307/mo ($109 base plus $99 for each additional inspector), while InspectReport AI is $9/mo flat per account. That is a difference of about $298/mo, or roughly $3,576 per year.

Does InspectReport AI charge per inspector?

No. Pricing is $9/mo flat per account, with no per-inspector fees and no promotional price that increases after an introductory period. Accounts, reports, and the live preview are free; the paid plan keeps PDF export after the free 7-day Pro trial.
