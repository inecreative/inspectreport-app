FAQ

Estimate questions, answered

Is it free to start?

Yes. Create a free account and build your first estimate right in the app — line items, your prices, your terms. Accounts, reports, estimates, and the live preview stay free. The first 5 pros get free full access — that offer is still open.

Do I need a credit card?

No. Every new account gets a 7-day free Pro trial with full access, including PDF export, and no card is required. After the trial, $9/month flat per account keeps PDF export; everything else stays free.

How does my customer approve the estimate?

Turn on the estimate's share link and text or email it to your customer. They open it — no account, no app — see the estimate with your company branding, tick any optional add-ons they want, and approve it with their name. The page answers with a big Approved confirmation, and you see the moment they view it and the moment they approve. One click then converts the approved estimate into a Job Report.

Can I use my own prices?

Yes. Customization is content, on a fixed clean layout: your own price book and line items, estimate templates you save by name, your default terms, exclusions, warranty, validity and deposit settings in your profile, your sections, and your company branding.

Do you email the estimate to my customer for me?

No — and that's deliberate. You copy the link and send it from your own phone or email, so the estimate comes from you, not from a tool your customer has never heard of. The page they open carries your company name and details; our name appears only as a tiny credit at the very bottom.

Does it replace Jobber or scheduling software?

No — honestly, it does not. InspectReport AI is the document, not dispatch: it does not do scheduling, dispatching, or running your whole operation. If you need full field-service management, keep that tool; use this for a clean, priced estimate and job documents.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro is $9/month flat per account with no per-user fees and no per-document fees, and you can cancel anytime. Your account, reports, and live preview stay free after you cancel.
