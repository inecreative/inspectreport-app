FAQ

Estimate questions, answered

Is it free to start?

Yes. Create a free account, send one real job (photos + notes), and your first estimate is built with you. Accounts, reports, and the live preview stay free. The first 5 pros get free full access — that offer is still open.

Do I need a credit card?

No. Every new account gets a 3-day free Pro trial with full access, including PDF export, and no card is required. After the trial, $49/month flat per account keeps PDF export; everything else stays free.

Is my first estimate really done with me?

Yes. Estimates are rolling out, and self-serve estimate automation is not live yet. Early pros send one real job — photos and rough notes — and the founder personally builds and checks that first estimate with you, using your prices and your terms.

Can I use my own prices?

Yes. Customization is content, on a fixed clean layout: your own price book and line items, estimate templates you save by name, your default terms, exclusions, warranty, validity and deposit settings in your profile, your sections, and your company branding.

Does it replace Jobber or scheduling software?

No — honestly, it does not. InspectReport AI is the document, not dispatch: it does not do scheduling, dispatching, or running your whole operation. If you need full field-service management, keep that tool; use this for a clean, priced estimate and job documents.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro is $49/month flat per account with no per-user fees and no per-document fees, and you can cancel anytime. Your account, reports, and live preview stay free after you cancel.
