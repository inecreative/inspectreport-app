FAQ

Questions, answered

What is InspectReport AI?

InspectReport AI is AI home inspection report software for home inspectors. Upload site photos, type short notes the way you'd say them on site, and it turns them into a structured inspection report with severity badges, repair-cost estimates, and PDF export.

Can AI write my home inspection report from photos?

Yes. Add a photo and a short note for each finding, and the AI structures it into a titled finding with a client-ready description, a suggested severity, and a repair-cost estimate. You review and edit everything in the live preview before any PDF is exported — you stay in control of the final report.

How much does InspectReport AI cost?

Starting is free: your account, reports, and the live preview cost nothing. Every new account gets a free 3-day Pro trial with full access including PDF export, no card required. After the trial, Pro is $49/month flat per account to keep PDF export.

Is there a free trial? Do I need a credit card?

Yes — every new account starts with a free 3-day Pro trial, and no credit card is required. You get full access during the trial, including PDF export. When the trial ends, reports and the live preview stay free; the $49/mo plan only keeps PDF export.

Does InspectReport AI charge per inspector?

No. Pricing is one flat $49/month per account — no per-inspector fees, no per-report fees, and no intro promo that doubles after 12 months. Several competing tools charge per inspector or per inspection, so their bill grows with your team; this one doesn't.

What is free, and what does the paid plan unlock?

Free forever: your account, creating reports, and the live preview. The paid Pro plan unlocks one thing — PDF export — after your free 3-day Pro trial ends. Everything else stays usable on the free account.

What does the finished PDF look like?

Exactly like the sample on this page: your property and client details (including your custom fields), an executive summary, and every finding with its photos, severity badge, and cost estimate, plus a total for repairs. You download it straight from the report with one click.

Who can see my reports and photos?

Only you. Your reports and photos are tied to your account sign-in, and other accounts can't see them. We don't sell your data, and your photos are used only to build your reports.
