FAQ

Questions, answered

How does the AI part actually work?

You type rough field notes — fragments are fine. The AI turns each note into a titled finding with a client-ready description, a suggested severity (Good, Monitor, Repair, or Urgent), and a ballpark repair-cost estimate. You stay in control: review, edit, or delete anything before the report goes out. If no AI key is configured on your account, a built-in structurer still does the basics.

What does the finished PDF look like?

Exactly like the sample on this page: your property and client details (including your custom fields), an executive summary, and every finding with its photos, severity badge, and cost estimate, plus a total for repairs. You download it straight from the report with one click.

Is the $49 per inspector?

No — $49/mo is flat per account, not per inspector. There’s no per-seat math, and there’s no intro promo that doubles after 12 months. The price you start at is the price.

Who can see my reports and photos?

Only you. Your reports and photos are tied to your account sign-in, and other accounts can’t see them. We don’t sell your data, and your photos are used only to build your reports.

When do I start paying?

Not yet. The beta is free and no card is required to join. When billing launches, the plan is $49/mo flat per account — we’ll email you before anything is charged, and you can decide then whether to continue.
