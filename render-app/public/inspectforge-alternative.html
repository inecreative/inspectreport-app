Honest fit check

Who should switch — and who shouldn’t

Switching makes sense if…

  • You pay InspectForge’s Business tier ($99/mo after promo) mainly for AI photo analysis and report writing.
  • Your InterNACHI promo year is ending and your bill is about to step up.
  • You want one flat price that does not change with headcount or tier, and you do not use the scheduling, CRM, or payments side of the platform.

Stay with InspectForge if…

  • You rely on InspectForge’s offline mobile apps, scheduling, CRM, payment collection, or agreements/e-signatures — InspectReport AI does not replace those; it is report-only and web-only.
  • You are a solo inspector on the Starter tier who does not need AI photo analysis: price no longer favors staying ($9/mo here vs $39/mo there after promo), but InspectForge’s trial is longer (30 days vs our 7) and the basic tier may already cover your workflow. Switching makes sense when you want the AI tier at a flat price, not when the basic tier already covers you.
  • You need template migration from another platform — InspectForge offers it free; InspectReport AI builds each report fresh from your photos and notes.

FAQ

InspectForge switchers ask

Is InspectReport AI an InspectForge alternative?

Yes, for report writing. It turns site photos and short notes into a structured inspection report with severity badges, repair-cost estimates, and PDF export. It is not a full platform: it does not do scheduling, CRM, payments, agreements, or offline mobile apps.

How much cheaper is it than InspectForge?

Cheaper at every tier. InspectReport AI is $9/month flat per account, with AI included. InspectForge’s basic Starter tier is $39/month after its promo year — $30/month more. The Business tier, where AI photo analysis lives, is $99/month after its promo year — $90/month more, about $1,080 a year. No InspectForge tier costs less than $9/month.

Does the price go up after a promo year?

No. InspectReport AI has no promo pricing: $9/month flat per account after the free trial. InspectForge’s InterNACHI pricing steps up after 12 months (Starter $25→$39, Pro $35→$69, Business $49→$99).

Is there a free trial? Do I need a credit card?

Every new account gets a free 7-day Pro trial with full access, including PDF export, and no credit card is required. After the trial, the account, reports, and live preview stay free; $9/month flat per account keeps PDF export. InspectForge’s trial is 30 days, also with no card required.

Can I try it before cancelling InspectForge?

Yes. Start free, use the 7-day Pro trial on a real report, and keep your InspectForge account until you have seen the finished report and PDF. There is nothing to migrate — each report is built from your photos and notes.
